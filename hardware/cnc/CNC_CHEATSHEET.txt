KEYPAD SYNTH REV A - CNC CHEAT SHEET (Genmitsu Cubiko + FlatCAM)
=================================================================
Board: 96 x 66 mm, rounded corners, single-sided, ALL COPPER ON THE BOTTOM (B_Cu)
Fits the 100 x 70 copper-clad, zero X/Y ~2 mm in. No wire jumpers.
Parts: ESP32-S3 SuperMini (2x9 female headers), 1.5" 128x128 SH1107 I2C OLED (1x4 female),
MAX98357A breakout (1x7 female), 4x4 tact keypad module + pot module on EDGE PADS.

EDGE PADS (new on this board)
  The keypad and the pot keep their right-angle header pins. They hang OFF the board, right side up,
  and their pins lie flat on long rectangular pads on the copper (bottom) side:
    - keypad: 8 pads on the BOTTOM edge, left -> right R1 R2 R3 R4 C1 C2 C3 C4 (as printed on the keypad)
    - pot:    3 pads on the RIGHT edge; middle pad = wiper. Swapping the outer legs only reverses the knob.
  Side view: the main board rests ON the pins, so the module sits ~1.5 mm below the main board and the
  key tops end up about level with the board top. Tin the pads, lay the pins on, reflow each one.
  Support the keypad with standoffs in its corner holes (or a base plate) - don't let 8 joints carry it.
  These pads have no holes, so there is nothing to drill for them.

BEFORE MILLING
  1. Print PRINT_1to1_fit_test.pdf at 100% (no "fit to page"). Check:
     - keypad: lay its pins on the R1..C4 pads - pitch and order (R1 on the LEFT seen from the top)
     - pot: middle pin on the "W" pad
     - OLED: 4 header pins on U3, body to the RIGHT of the header (display up)
     - SuperMini (top view, USB-C pointing LEFT at the left edge):
         top row     5V GND 3V3 13 12 11 10 9 8   (left -> right)
         bottom row  TX RX 1 2 3 4 5 6 7          (left -> right)
     - Amp: header row along its top edge, VIN on the LEFT, speaker terminal at the bottom
  2. Mounting holes: the 4 corners (TL is next to the SuperMini's USB end, BL is under the amp -
     put those screws in before plugging the modules in).

G-CODE - ALREADY GENERATED (FlatCAM ran headless on the studio PC, 2026-10-02)
  Run in this order:
  1_isolation_0.1vbit.nc      20 deg V-bit, Z -0.05 (go -0.07 if traces don't separate)
  2_holes_1.0drill.nc         1.0 mm drill, all 29 component holes
  3_mounting_2.0drill.nc      2.0 mm drill, 4 mounting pilots -> open to 3.2 by hand (M3)
  4_outline_score_0.1vbit.nc  V-bit scores the outline at Z -0.07, snap/cut out by hand
  Drill files are ALREADY spindle-patched (fix_drill_spinup.py, .orig copies kept).
  The empty bottom-right area isn't connected to ground, so it stays as floating copper - that's fine.
  To regenerate after a board change: FlatCAM > File > Scripting > Run Script (Shift+S)
  > cnc/load_and_mirror.tcl, then  python fix_drill_spinup.py 2_holes_1.0drill.nc 3_mounting_2.0drill.nc

BIT CHANGE ROUTINE (V-bit -> 1.0 -> 2.0 -> V-bit)  - same as the other boards
  1. Swap the bit (file ends at Z15)            2. DON'T touch X/Y zero
  3. Re-zero Z by hand (paper) with the new bit  4. No height map / probing with drills
  5. Spindle on full, check it spins, run. Files start M03 S10000 + 3 s pause.

FIRMWARE PIN MAP (ESP32-S3 SuperMini) - all 15 header GPIOs used
  OLED I2C: SDA 9, SCL 8 (addr 0x3C), powered from 3V3
  Keypad: rows R1-R4 = 2 3 4 5 (drive LOW one at a time), columns C1-C4 = 6 7 13 12 (INPUT_PULLUP)
  Pot: wiper 10 (ADC1), high leg = GPIO11 driven HIGH, low leg = GND
  Amp (I2S out): LRC 1, BCLK 44 (RX), DIN 43 (TX), VIN from 5V
  Arduino: "ESP32S3 Dev Module", USB CDC On Boot = ENABLED (required: TX/RX feed the amp)
  Test sketch: firmware/keypad_bringup/keypad_bringup.ino (compiles clean)

REGENERATING THE BOARD
  python tools/gen_board.py  then  "C:\Program Files\KiCad\10.0\bin\python.exe" tools/fill_sync.py
  then kicad-cli ERC + DRC (--schematic-parity --severity-all). Rev A: 0 / 0 / 0.
